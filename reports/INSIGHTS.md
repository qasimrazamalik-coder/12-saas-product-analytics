
# Decision Brief — SaaS Product Analytics

## What the data says

- **Enterprise** leads revenue at **$2,535,150**.
- **Enterprise** has the highest observed risk rate at **12.6%**.
- Portfolio revenue is **$9,972,550** across 1,200 records.

## Recommended actions

1. Protect the leading segment with capacity and service-level monitoring.
2. Audit the high-risk segment using the intervention query in `sql/analysis.sql`.
3. Track quality and risk together; optimizing volume alone can hide operational deterioration.
4. Re-run monthly and investigate any segment whose risk rate rises while quality declines.

## Limits
